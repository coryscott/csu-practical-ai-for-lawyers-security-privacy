Examples from Wiz CIRT’s report on AI-driven cloud attacks

CAPABILITY

Proficiency across platforms

Native tools across cloud, Kubernetes, CI/CD, identity and databases. Examples included AWS Systems Manager commands and GCP service-account token creation.

Rapid trial and error

Reading errors, revising scripts and retrying within minutes. The agent corrected identity-setup errors and, when Kubernetes blocked access to Secrets, searched ConfigMaps instead.

CAPACITY

Operational tempo

Hundreds or thousands of actions per hour, sustained for long periods without the pauses typical of a human operator.

Simultaneous activity across platforms

GCP and AWS operations ran in parallel. Five concurrent CI/CD and GitOps workstreams harvested secrets, mapped access, tried credentials, attempted service exploitation and expanded compute.

Confidential Discovery
and AI Use

  • Producing parties are concerned that receiving counsel or litigants will upload confidential discovery to AI tools.
  • Depending on the service and its terms, the provider may store the material, allow human access, use it for training or process it for other purposes.
  • Parties need enforceable safeguards for confidentiality and use of the material in the case.
  • Courts and parties are writing specific AI-use conditions into protective orders.

Self-represented litigants also use AI for research, drafting and case preparation, expanding access to assistance while raising the same confidentiality questions.

Corent v. AWS:
agreed conditions for AI use

THE UNDERLYING DISPUTE

Corent alleges that AWS products and services infringe its patents. The dispute involves commercial competitors, with discovery expected to expose proprietary source code, technical documentation, and sensitive business information.

Stipulated protective order · September 2026 · Applies to all discovery material

01

Commercial terms

Qualifying enterprise/commercial tier and documented commitments.

02

Training and access

No general-model training or unauthorized access; vendor human review tightly limited.

03

Matter isolation

Case-specific history and retrieval context may be retained. No cross-matter reuse.

04

Deletion and proof

Deletion rights, deadlines and written certification; retain contracts and terms.

05

Other tools

Advance notice, an objection period and court resolution of unresolved objections.

06

Source code

No AI use of material marked HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL—SOURCE CODE.

Discussion: could this negotiated approach become a model for other protective orders?

Corent Technology, Inc. v. Amazon Web Services, Inc., No. 8:26-cv-00194-JVS-(DFMx), Stipulated Protective Order § 12, at 28–34 (C.D. Cal. Sept. 28, 2026), ECF No. 53.
